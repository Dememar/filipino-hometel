Our Rooms

Choose the perfect room for your stay

Standard Room

The Standard Room Hut is cozy and welcoming, designed with natural bamboo accents and warm lighting. It’s perfect for travelers who want a simple yet comfortable stay surrounded by a rustic Filipino atmosphere.

  • Mid‑size double bed with bamboo headboard
  • Private bathroom with eco-friendly toiletries
  • Wooden nightstand with woven abaca lamp for soft ambient light
  • Small window with bamboo blinds, letting in natural light and greenery views

Deluxe Room

The Deluxe Room Hut offers more space and comfort, blending earthy tones with handcrafted details. It’s ideal for couples or small families who want a relaxing tropical retreat with extra room to unwind.

  • Queen‑sized wooden bed with accent pillows in warm earthy colors
  • Spacious layout with polished wooden floors
  • Large window or glass door with light curtains, opening to greenery outside
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi and cable TV

Premium Room

The Premium Room Hut is the most luxurious option, combining traditional Filipino design with modern resort touches. It’s perfect for guests seeking a serene, high‑end tropical experience with breathtaking ocean views.

  • King-size bed with premium bedding
  • Private bathroom with spa-like amenities
  • Four‑poster canopy bed draped in sheer white curtains
  • Balcony with ocean view, palm trees, and natural scenery
